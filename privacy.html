Hermes Drive Reader - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-17

  • Users: only the owner of this project, signing in with their own Google account.
  • Data accessed: files in the owner's Google Drive, read-only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly). The app never creates, modifies, or deletes Drive files.
  • Use and storage: files are copied only to the owner's own computer and storage devices for personal archiving.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking access: the owner can remove the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
